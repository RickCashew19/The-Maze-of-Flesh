INTRODUCTION

Overview:
Maze of Flesh is a top-down pixel survival horror game inspired by Pac-Man
and The Binding of Isaac. The player must navigate a procedurally generated
maze filled with twisted, flesh-like corridors and lurking monsters to find all keys
and escape.

Purpose:
To deliver an atmospheric and replayable horror experience while showcasing
Java programming and object-oriented design through procedural maze
generation and AI-driven enemy behavior.

Target Audience:
Players who enjoy dark, mystery-driven games with simple but challenging
mechanics especially fans of indie horror and retro-style games.
